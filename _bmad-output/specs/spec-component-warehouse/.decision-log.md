## 2026-09-30 — update after adversarial fact-check

An independent reviewer checked every claim against the code. Five critical findings were
verified and accepted:

1. **Evidence moves out of docstrings.** The admission audit scans all source strings, so consumer names in docstrings would fail it (`admission/source.py:125`). Evidence now lives in `docs/component-warehouse/evidence.yaml`.
2. **The weighting rule is corrected.** Per-micro-batch `/sum(w)` breaks micro-batch invariance. The rule is now:
   - class weights keep native loss semantics (`child_mind` exact);
   - sample weights use `mean(w·l)` with training-role mean-1 weights (`child_mind/sequence` exact).
3. **Single model input.** Predictor, export and `predict_step` accept only `x`. v1 keeps that contract, matching the CMI spec's "no widened Predictor API". Model-side validity and modality presence become declared channels of `x`. Multi-field inputs are roadmapped.
4. **CAP-7 is rewritten.** It adopts the reviewer's wording: project-constructed roots, one native `Trainer.fit`, invariants only, no task/mode argument. An ADR must define "universal trainer" (a DSio-owned loop or task-dispatching entry point) and amend the spine. CAP-7 functions are spine functions, not Components.
5. **The `dsio.eval` import contract holds.** The evaluate/infer function (#27) is dropped. Array assembly (#5) lives in `dsio.data`, and consumers call `evaluate`/`predict` directly.

Important findings accepted:
- Counts are now per callable (#4 split into 4a–4c; #8 into 8a–8b; #9 into 9a–9b; #13 rises to 2 uses; #14 re-based).
- #6 is dropped because it fails the depth test.
- The collator supersession is acknowledged and becomes the depth constraint on #3.
- cmi-seq streaming evaluation and window → entity aggregation (#28) are deferred, and CAP-6 is narrowed.
- Parity is redefined: metrics rather than identities, committed golden contract metrics, declared computation changes, recorded hardware, and a testable definition of "flake resolved".
- Dataset, collator and fitted-value provenance is now required, and config/runtime collisions raise.
- The catalog's public surface, registers and generator location are defined.
- The success signal uses a syntax-tree check plus a non-ingestion line reduction, replacing the gameable ≤300 file target.
- "Catalog alone" becomes "wheel, README and catalog, outside the repo".
